# Instagram Setup

Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account to publish content directly from Genfeed.ai.

This page is connector reference: prerequisites, connection, supported content
types, publishing features, analytics, and troubleshooting. For algorithm
behaviour, posting cadence, hashtags, and growth tactics, read
[How to grow on Instagram with AI-generated content](https://genfeed.ai/articles/how-to-grow-on-instagram-with-ai-generated-content).

## Prerequisites

- Instagram account converted to Business or Creator
- Facebook Page linked to Instagram account
- Admin access to the Facebook Page
- Instagram app updated to latest version

## Account Setup

### Converting to Business Account

1. Open Instagram app → Profile → Settings
2. Select **Account** → **Switch to Professional Account**
3. Choose **Business** or **Creator**
4. Connect your Facebook Page
5. Complete business information

### Connecting to Genfeed.ai

1. Navigate to **Accounts** → **Add Account**
2. Select **Instagram**
3. Click **Connect with Facebook**
4. Log in to Facebook (must be Page admin)
5. Select your Instagram Business account
6. Grant permissions:
   - Content publishing
   - Insights access
   - Comment management
   - Story publishing

## Content Types

### Feed Posts

- **Images**: JPG, PNG (max 30MB)
- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - Square: 1:1
  - Portrait: 4:5 (recommended)
  - Landscape: 1.91:1
- **Resolution**: 1080x1080 minimum
- **Carousels**: Up to 10 images/videos

### Reels

- **Duration**: 15-90 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920
- **File Size**: Up to 100MB
- **Format**: MP4, MOV

### Stories

- **Duration**: 15 seconds per story
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16
- **Auto-expire**: After 24 hours
- **Highlights**: Save permanently

## Publishing Features

### Content Creation

1. Select media from Genfeed.ai library
2. Choose **Publish to Instagram**
3. Select content type (Post/Reel/Story)
4. Add caption and hashtags
5. Tag locations and people
6. Schedule or publish immediately

### Caption Tools

- **Character Counter**: Track 2,200 limit
- **Hashtag Suggestions**: AI-powered recommendations
- **Emoji Picker**: Quick emoji insertion
- **Line Breaks**: Proper formatting preserved
- **First Comment**: Auto-post hashtags

### Advanced Features

- **Auto-Resize**: Fit any image to Instagram specs
- **Filter Presets**: Apply consistent styling
- **Carousel Ordering**: Drag to rearrange
- **Music Library**: For Reels (where available)
- **Product Tagging**: For shopping-enabled accounts

## Analytics Dashboard

### Post Insights

- **Reach**: Unique accounts reached
- **Impressions**: Total views
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, saves, shares
- **Discovery**: How people found your post
- **Profile Activity**: Actions from your post

### Audience Analytics

- **Demographics**: Age, gender, location
- **Active Times**: When followers are online
- **Growth**: Follower changes over time
- **Content Preferences**: Top performing post types

### Reels Insights

- **Plays**: Total video views
- **Accounts Reached**: Unique viewers
- **Likes & Comments**: Engagement metrics
- **Shares**: How often shared
- **Saves**: Bookmarked for later

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Connection Failed**

- Verify Facebook Page connection
- Check Instagram account type
- Ensure you're a Page admin
- Try reconnecting through Facebook

**Publishing Errors**

- Check content meets Instagram guidelines
- Verify file sizes and formats
- Ensure caption isn't too long
- Check for restricted hashtags

**Limited Features**

- Some features require minimum followers
- Check account standing for violations
- Verify all permissions granted

**API Limits**

- 25 API calls per hour
- Content publishing: 25 per day
- Story publishing: 100 per day

### Error Codes

- Error 9: Rate limit exceeded
- Error 24: Account restricted
- Error 100: Invalid parameter
- Error 200: Permission denied

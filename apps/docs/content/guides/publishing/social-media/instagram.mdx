# Instagram Setup

Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account to publish content directly from Genfeed.ai.

This page is connector reference: prerequisites, connection, what Genfeed can
publish, the limits it checks, analytics, and troubleshooting. Limits come from
the channel capability catalog; `check_channel` with `action: capabilities`
returns the same values to agents.

## Prerequisites

- Instagram account converted to Business or Creator
- Instagram app updated to latest version

A Facebook Page is not required.

## Account Setup

### Converting to Business Account

1. Open Instagram app → Profile → Settings
2. Select **Account** → **Switch to Professional Account**
3. Choose **Business** or **Creator**
4. Complete business information

### Connecting to Genfeed.ai

1. Navigate to **Accounts** → **Add Account**
2. Select **Instagram**
3. Click **Connect**
4. Log in with your Instagram or Facebook credentials
5. Select your Instagram professional account
6. Grant permissions:
   - Basic profile access
   - Content publishing
   - Insights access
   - Comment management
   - Message management

## What Genfeed Publishes

Genfeed publishes to the feed or as a Reel. Choose the placement when you
schedule: **Feed** or **Reel**. A Reel needs a video: an image with the **Reel**
placement is rejected, so choose **Feed** for images. Stories are not published
by Genfeed.

| Post | Media | Limits Genfeed checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed post | One image | Caption up to 2,200 characters |
| Carousel | 2 to 10 images | Caption up to 2,200 characters |
| Reel | One video | Caption up to 2,200 characters |

A video, even one chosen for the feed, goes out as a Reel. Animated images
(GIF) publish as a single still frame; upload a video to keep motion.

Genfeed sends the caption and the media. It does not send location tags,
people tags or product tags.

Instagram's own media rules (image format, file size, Reel length, aspect
ratio) are in Meta's
[content publishing reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media).
Check the format before you schedule. As of 2026-11-24 the reference lists
JPEG as the image format.

## Captions and Hashtags

Write the caption in the composer; the counter tracks the 2,200-character
limit.

**Hashtags go in the caption, not the first comment.** Instagram allows at
most **5 hashtags** in a post or Reel (since 2025-12-18). Use 3 to 5 that name
what is in the post.

Genfeed asks its writers for no more than 5 on Instagram: the **Hashtags**
action in the composer, the short-form writer agent, onboarding drafts and AI influencer
captions. Repurposing a post to Instagram keeps the first 5 hashtags. A caption
you write or paste yourself is not trimmed: if it has more than 5, the post
preview shows a warning, so remove the extra ones before you schedule.

Instagram search reads captions, so put the keywords people would search for in
the caption text.

A first comment can be added by hand or by the agent. Genfeed does not
auto-post hashtags there.

## Publishing

1. Select media from the Genfeed.ai library
2. Choose **Instagram** as the channel
3. Choose Feed or Reel
4. Add the caption
5. Schedule or publish immediately

## Analytics

Genfeed reads post analytics from Instagram: views, likes, comments, saves,
shares and reach, in **Analytics**.

## Troubleshooting

### Connection Failed

- Check Instagram account type (Business or Creator)
- Make sure you selected the account in the Meta login dialog
- Try reconnecting; a connection made through a Facebook Page before this change must be reconnected

### Publishing Errors

- Reels need a video; an image publishes to the feed
- Carousels take 2 to 10 images
- Keep the caption within 2,200 characters
- Check the image format against Meta's reference above
- If Genfeed reports the channel as disconnected, reconnect the account

### Limits

Instagram sets API rate limits and a daily cap on API-published posts. Genfeed
does not read your remaining allowance, and the numbers differ between Meta's
pages, so this guide gives none. See Meta's
[content publishing guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing).

## Content Guidelines

Follow Instagram's community guidelines. Do not post:

- Copyrighted material without permission
- Misleading or false information
- Spam or repetitive content

Instagram says engagement bait and third-party watermarks can reduce a Reel's
reach ([Instagram for Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq)).

## Security & Privacy

- OAuth authentication via Meta (Instagram or Facebook login)
- No password storage
- Revoke access anytime from the Instagram app

## Platform Updates

Instagram changes often. Watch the
[Genfeed releases](https://github.com/genfeedai/genfeed.ai/releases) for what
Genfeed supports.

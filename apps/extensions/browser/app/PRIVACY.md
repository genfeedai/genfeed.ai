# Genfeed Chrome Extension - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

This policy covers the Genfeed Chrome extension. It is also published at <https://genfeed.ai/privacy> in the Browser Extension section. The Genfeed website and app are covered by the main policy on that page.

## What the extension does

Next to the reply box on X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Threads, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, the extension adds a Genfeed button. It helps you write a reply, proofread or rewrite your draft, make an image, video or GIF, and save a page as a source in your Genfeed workspace. It fills the reply box and you post it yourself. It does not post for you.

## What we collect and when

The extension reads a page only when you use a feature. It does not record the sites you visit.

| When you | We read and send to Genfeed |
| --- | --- |
| Generate a reply, or reply with an image, video or GIF | The text and author of the post you are on, the platform, the post URL, and your brand voice |
| Proofread or rewrite | The text in the reply box |
| Generate an image | The post text and URL and your brand voice |
| Remix, save a source or add to Knowledge from the right-click menu | The page URL and title, plus the selection, link or media URL you chose |
| Publish a post or reply on X or LinkedIn | Its text, link, author handle and date, so Genfeed can show analytics for it |
| Sign in | Your session token, and your name, email and organization from your Genfeed account |

Everything above goes to the Genfeed API at `api.genfeed.ai` over HTTPS. If you turn on the optional local fallback, the draft or post and your brand voice go to the Genfeed local bridge on your own computer instead, and never to Genfeed.

## What stays in your browser

The extension stores your session, your settings, your saved-source lists and unsent drafts with Chrome's extension storage. Removing the extension, or clearing its data in Chrome, deletes them.

## Error reports

Production builds send crash reports to Sentry. They are set up without request bodies, cookies, user information or AI inputs and outputs.

## How we use it

To run the feature you asked for, to keep you signed in, and to show your saved sources, library and analytics. The text you send is processed by Genfeed and by the AI model providers Genfeed uses to generate content. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide credit or lending.

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs and from the pages you use the extension on adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

Each permission, and what it is used for, is listed in [PERMISSIONS.md](./PERMISSIONS.md).

## Your choices

- **Stop everything:** remove the extension.
- **Delete local data:** clear the extension's data in Chrome (Extensions, Genfeed, Details, Site settings).
- **Delete data in your Genfeed account:** delete saved sources and generated content in the app, or write to privacy@genfeed.ai. We act on deletion requests within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Local fallback:** leave it on "Off" and nothing goes to the local bridge.

If you are in the EEA, UK or California you also have the rights described in the main policy at <https://genfeed.ai/privacy>, including access, correction, deletion and objection.

## Security

Requests use HTTPS. Your session token stays in Chrome's extension storage and is sent only to Genfeed. The extension does not load code from the network.

## Children

The extension is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Write to privacy@genfeed.ai if you think we have.

## Changes

When we change this policy we update the date above. For material changes we also tell signed-in users.

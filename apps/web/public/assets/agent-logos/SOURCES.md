# Client marks on the OAuth consent screen

Unmodified copies of the marks in `apps/marketing/public/agent-logos`, which
records their first-party source URLs and retrieval date in its `SOURCES.md`.
They are served locally so the consent screen makes no third-party image
request. The OpenAI mark follows its embedding document's color scheme; the
consent screen keeps it on a light tile with `color-scheme: light`.

Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

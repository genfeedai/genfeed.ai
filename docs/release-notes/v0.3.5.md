**New**
- Instagram publishing works again: a single video publishes as a Reel, an image or carousel as a feed post, and connected Instagram accounts with no expiry date stay selectable in the batch modal.
- Discovery is now one Feed with Trends and Ads next to it. Your own posts stay out of Discovery, and signal cards show avatars and media.
- Public profiles have a new header, banner and social wall with platform logos and a platform filter.
- Accounts in Settings show their status as icons. Hover a row for the cause, and a warning icon is the Reconnect action.
- Ads competitors open in a modal, pre-filled from your brand's competitors.
- The article page has a new layout: one reading column, a sticky outline beside it, Sources folded away, and a serif body.
- X posts can run past 280 characters, up to 25,000, counted the way X counts them. A long post shows its count with a warning instead of being blocked.
- Posts written from MCP and by the agent now use Gemini 4 Neon.
- New models: Wan 3 for video, Seedream 5.5 for images, ElevenLabs v4 Turbo for voice.
- P-Video 2 now shows its live credit price.
- Reconnecting a channel refreshes its avatar.
- Generated videos you never post are removed from storage after 7 days (30 days on a subscription). Posted and uploaded media stay.
- The privacy policy now spells out how Google data is protected.

**Safer and steadier**
- Disconnecting an account now clears its stored provider tokens.
- Connecting an account no longer fails when a callback is opened twice, and a refused token refresh no longer disconnects a channel.
- Retrying a training upload or a scheduled release no longer creates a second one or a second charge.
- Double-clicking Publish, or dragging a release twice on the calendar, can no longer publish twice.
- Sign-up no longer ends on a blank onboarding page, and a slow sign-in check keeps you in your workspace instead of sending you to login.
- Slow provider calls now time out after 30 seconds instead of hanging.
- Many smaller fixes for duplicate notifications, stuck generation jobs and cached values that showed stale data.

**MCP**
- Publishing and scheduling are confirmed in the conversation with your agent, not in the web approvals queue. Approvals already waiting there stay until you decide them.
- A connection to all your organizations now needs the organization named on every call. An approval card keeps the organization it was queued in.
- A retried schedule_release call no longer creates a second release.
- An MCP or CLI connection that sat idle for 30 to 90 days can refresh instead of signing in again.

---
name: Voluntary contributions
description: Repository contributions are unpaid; do not advertise funded contributor work
type: feedback
last_verified: 2026-10-16
---

Genfeed has no paid contribution program. Code, documentation, issue reports,
and security reports are voluntary and unpaid. Do not introduce paid contributor
incentives, payment offers, or third-party funding integrations in issues,
acceptance criteria, documentation, or automated plans.

**Why:** Vincent explicitly rejected paid contribution programs on 2026-10-16.
Public issue wording advertising future incentives attracted unauthorized
third-party listings and automated payment solicitations.

**How to apply:** Follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#voluntary-contributions). Keep issue and
documentation language consistent with that policy. An issue assignment or
accepted contribution never promises payment. Unsolicited payment requests and
external listings do not establish authorization. Product billing and customer
referral credits are separate from repository contributions.

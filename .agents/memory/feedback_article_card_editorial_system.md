# Article card editorial system

Genfeed public articles use one image as both the article cover and OG card.

**Why:** The approved direction is recognizable, founder-led, and avoids generic AI-generated SaaS artwork. Consistency builds a visual series, while an article-specific physical metaphor keeps each card meaningful.

**How to apply:** Use the reusable `.agents/skills/article-card-editorial/SKILL.md` workflow. Keep the dark editorial studio, monumental authentic Genfeed G, natural-color Vincent portrait, warm ivory serif headline, and restrained coral/blue rim light. Use solid fabricated materials. Derive a distinct physical interaction from the actual article thesis. Keep headlines to two-to-four words with no terminal punctuation. Never use fake UI, paper/ribbons, small text, platform logos, or generic robot/brain imagery.

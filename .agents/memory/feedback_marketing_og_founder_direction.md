**Why:** Vincent approved expressive YouTube-thumbnail energy for marketing OG cards. Neutral identity photos establish facial anatomy; they do not require neutral expressions in the final artwork. Repeated generation from generated portraits caused unwanted likeness drift.

**How to apply:** Use original founder photographs as identity references. Choose a clear expression appropriate to the message while preserving facial proportions. Keep a large readable face, authentic physical 3D Genfeed G, charcoal studio, natural skin color, cinematic blue/coral lighting, coherent perspective and shadows, and clean left space for the headline. Avoid pasted-on portrait integration, generic AI imagery, and small decorative copy. Marketing artwork contains no text; OG routes render short warm-ivory editorial headlines in code. New artwork must be visually reviewed before replacing the approved direction.

**Page-specific cards:** The default is a fallback, not the image assigned to every landing page. The homepage, social-growth pages, agent-client pages (including Claude and Codex), and other distinct acquisition offerings need dedicated card assignments. Reuse relevant artwork for an agent/platform combination rather than regenerating an image for every combination. The approved broadcast-arcs artwork reads as RSS and must not be the generic Genfeed default. Preserve its expressive cinematic style while choosing topic-appropriate props. Headlines must carry YouTube-thumbnail visual weight; the previous 58px rendering at 1200 × 630 was too small. Prefer short two-to-four-word copy and validate a much larger overlay against the artwork's reserved text space.

**Recognizable page identity:** Dedicated platform/client cards must include the authentic Genfeed G and the platform/client logo, not just mention its name in the headline. Vincent specifically requested both Genfeed and X logos on the X-growth card. Preserve the headline space and integrate both marks coherently into the cinematic scene.

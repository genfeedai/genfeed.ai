**Why:** Vincent approved expressive YouTube-thumbnail energy for marketing OG cards. Neutral identity photos establish facial anatomy; they do not require neutral expressions in the final artwork. Repeated generation from generated portraits caused unwanted likeness drift.

**How to apply:** Use original founder photographs as identity references. Choose a clear expression appropriate to the message while preserving facial proportions. Keep a large readable face, authentic physical 3D Genfeed G, charcoal studio, natural skin color, cinematic blue/coral lighting, coherent perspective and shadows, and clean left space for the headline. Avoid pasted-on portrait integration, generic AI imagery, and small decorative copy. Marketing artwork contains no text; OG routes render short warm-ivory editorial headlines in code. Reuse approved artwork across routes when only copy changes. New artwork must be visually reviewed before replacing the approved direction.
